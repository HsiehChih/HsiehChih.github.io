About the Lab

IRSL builds intelligent, resilient systems with a simple premise: AI should not stay confined to idealized lab conditions. It should remain dependable for decision-making and task execution even in the real world, where uncertainty is the norm and information is often incomplete. We believe the real value of agentic AI lies in applying it to domains where safety and efficiency genuinely matter, such as healthcare operations, civil infrastructure, and information security, and in demonstrating resilience there: the ability to perform reliably under disruption, limited resources, or a changing environment.

Our research spans five main directions. In agentic AI and multi-agent frameworks, we design systems that reason, plan, and act autonomously. In healthcare operations research, we focus on routing, resource allocation, and dispatch for mobile screening stations, helping healthcare capacity hold up under pressure. In BIM/MEP engineering, we develop automated clash detection and resolution methods built on IFC models, speeding up coordination between design and construction. In medical AI and clinical NLP, we study how to extract structured information from clinical text while carefully handling sensitive health data. In LLM agent security, we examine the jailbreak attacks large language model agent systems can face and develop corresponding defenses. These five directions may look scattered, but they share one thread: making intelligent systems trustworthy in environments that are real, and often imperfect.

Our work has been published in journals including Indoor Air, Advanced Engineering Informatics, Journal of Building Engineering, IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics, and Automation in Construction, and the lab holds two R.O.C. invention patents. We also partner with Kaohsiung Medical University Chung-Ho Memorial Hospital, applying AI to clinical settings such as structuring medical record text and parsing unstructured cancer case reports, and currently carry out an NSTC-funded project, "Agentic AI-Assisted Engineering Coordination and Resilient Resource Dispatch." In how we operate, IRSL centers on mentoring graduate and undergraduate students through real research problems, working across disciplines with faculty and institutions in architecture, civil engineering, and medicine so students build research skills while gaining experience solving real-world problems.

Research Focus

Agentic AI

Multi-agent frameworks for autonomous reasoning, planning, and domain-specific task execution.

Healthcare Operations Research

Optimization and routing for mobile screening, resource allocation, and epidemic response.

BIM / MEP Engineering

Automated clash detection and resolution for IFC-based building information models.

Medical AI / Clinical NLP

Clinical text understanding and sensitive health information handling.

LLM Agent Security

Jailbreak attacks and defenses in LLM-based agent systems.
