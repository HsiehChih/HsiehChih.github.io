About

I am a faculty member in the Department of Electrical Engineering at National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology (NKUST), Taiwan, and principal investigator of the Intelligent Resilience Systems Laboratory (IRSL). My research centers on agentic AI systems and their application to resilience-critical domains, including healthcare operations, civil infrastructure, and information security.
